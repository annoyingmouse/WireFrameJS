Stage 1 — Learn

What makes a question open or closed?

Open questions
Invite a full, personal answer. There's no single right response — they unlock thinking and conversation.
"What did you enjoy most about it?"
"How did that make you feel?"
"What would you do differently?"
Closed questions
Can be answered with yes, no, or a single fact. They confirm or retrieve — they don't open things up.
"Did you enjoy it?"
"Were you upset?"
"Would you change it?"

A useful test: if someone could answer in one word, it's probably closed. Open questions start with words like how, what, why, tell me about, describe — they can't be shrugged off.
